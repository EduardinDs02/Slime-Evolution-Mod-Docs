Habitats and Comfort

Player guide · Care and capture · Species gallery

Use this list to choose a floor for each of the 47 slime species. These are the default comfort preferences, including inherited habitat blocks for hybrids. The gallery also shows the floor and biomes on each species card. Preferred biomes improve comfort; they do not imply that an acquired species spawns there naturally.

How comfort works

The slime must stand on a listed block. Placing that block nearby or in a wall does not grant the floor bonus. Any one accepted block gives the same bonus; combining several types does not stack it. Other block variants count only if listed. Water, food, decorations, and light do not provide a general comfort bonus.

Environment Comfort target before penalties
Neither preferred floor nor preferred biome 30
Preferred biome only 60
Preferred floor only 65
Preferred floor and preferred biome 95

Comfort moves toward this target by up to two points per loaded second. Keep the slime in its enclosure while it adjusts. Crouch and interact with an empty hand to check its current needs.

More than eight evolution slimes in the local four-block check subtracts 20 from the target; the slime itself counts. A fire-immune slime in water, rain, or bubbles, or a freezing-immune slime on fire, receives a 50-point penalty. The final environmental target is clamped to 0–100.

For example, Ancestral standing on Purpur Block, End Stone Bricks, or Block of Gold tends toward 65 comfort. In End Highlands, that becomes 95 before penalties. Mixing those three floors provides no additional bonus.
