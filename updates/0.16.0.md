# Slime Evolution 0.16.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

## Companion combat postures

The bond panel adds **Passive**, **Defensive** (default), and **Aggressive**, independently of movement commands.

Defensive responds to attacks on the caretaker or slime and assists against the caretaker's recent attack target. Aggressive also seeks visible hostiles within 16 blocks. Passive does not automatically attack or retaliate. Caretakers, companions, tame animals, and protected players remain excluded. Postures persist through saves, capsules, and metamorphoses. Labels and tooltips are available in English and Portuguese.

Companion contact now damages the selected mob, extending vanilla slime contact behavior beyond players and iron golems.

## Five followers

Each caretaker can have five Follow reservations across all dimensions. Unloaded followers retain their slots. Capturing a follower or changing its movement command frees a slot. Capsules and older saves cannot exceed the cap: excess followers switch to Wait while keeping their other data.
