## GeckoLib and SolarPunk presentation

Models adopt GeckoLib animation for movement, feeding, damage, transformation, and boss actions. **GeckoLib 4.9.3 becomes a required separate mod on both client and server.** Layered gel and elemental materials, original textures, equipment, and interfaces develop the SolarPunk visual direction.
