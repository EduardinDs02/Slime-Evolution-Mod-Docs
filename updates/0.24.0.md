# 0.24.0 — Expanded lineages

Adds sixteen acquired species: six hybrids, six environmental metamorphoses, and four advanced evolutions. Includes unique companion abilities, original model variants and atlases, bilingual Slimepedia entries and a Phoenix defeat prevention cooldown shared by its keeper. The complete roster contains 63 species, 82 breeding recipes and 24 metamorphoses.
