# Slime Evolution 0.18.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

## Keep your team together

Follow receives faster hopping and more frequent path updates outside combat. Ancestral and Celestial can follow in the air with real collision-aware navigation. A follower more than 32 blocks away can catch up by teleporting to safe, dry, supported ground near its caretaker in the same dimension. Unsafe space, combat, exhaustion, and unavailable or unloaded entities do not bypass the checks. The five-follower cap remains.

## Defeat instead of permanent loss

Bonded companions fall into a 10-second defeated state on lethal ordinary damage. Three orbiting stars mark recovery; they stay still and stop attacking and working. They recover with half health and brief protection while retaining their identity, bond, training, orders, and belongings. Recovery persists across saves and pauses while unloaded. Wild slimes and administrative removal retain normal death behavior.

[Movement, teleport, and recovery rules](../guides/COMPANIONS.md).

## Find the right floor

The [habitat reference](../guides/HABITATS.md) lists all 47 species, accepted floor blocks, and comfort biomes. Each gallery card now includes these preferences and a direct link. Inherited tags are expanded into block names. The guide explains floor contact, non-stacking bonuses, gradual adjustment, and crowding penalties.

Update the mod on both client and server. GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
