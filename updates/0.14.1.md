## Online guide and panoramic banner

The Slimepedia gains an Online guide button, using Minecraft’s address confirmation dialog and preserving book search and selection when returning. The guide supports topic navigation, search, a gallery, mobile layout, and offline distribution. A real-game panoramic banner features eight species, six items, and three machines.
