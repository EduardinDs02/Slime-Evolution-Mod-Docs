## A Slimepedia for every new player

Each player receives one Slimepedia on their first login to a world, with persistent delivery tracking across death, reconnection, and dimension changes. A full inventory drops the book at the player’s feet; an existing carried book avoids duplication. Existing worlds receive delivery on the first login after updating. The original crafting recipe remains available.

A new Minecraft advancement tab adds **16 milestones**, with English and Portuguese text: obtain the book and equipment; discover the first species and collections of 10 and 47; feed, analyze, breed, and transform slimes; defeat each boss and all four. Research milestones can recognize existing journal progress; boss milestones require new player-credited victories.
