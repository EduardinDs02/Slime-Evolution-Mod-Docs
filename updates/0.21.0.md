# Slime Evolution 0.21.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

- Fixes all four pedestals: side clicks work, and summoning finds a free position when the player or followers occupy the center. Permission, space, and Peaceful failures provide feedback without consuming the item.
- Companions continue basic attacks between special skills. Defensive finishes engaged fights, Aggressive avoids unnecessary target changes, pursuit tolerates brief obstructions, and wounded bonded slimes do not use wild docile panic. Skill costs, ally protection, and Passive stance remain enforced.
- Rebuilds all six structures with larger layouts, deeper facades, galleries, domes, glazing, interiors, boardwalks, and landscaping. Materials, routes to rewards, and ritual spaces are validated in Minecraft. [Gallery and references](../guides/SPECIAL_STRUCTURES.md).

The new builds appear in new chunks; existing generated builds are not replaced. Update client and server. GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
