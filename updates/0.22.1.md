# Slime Evolution 0.22.1

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

- Fixes parts of the same building crossing occupied spaces: the L-shaped library junction, the observatory interior, and the Ancestral Chamber's spires and aisle roofs.
- Connects roofs, domes, stained glass, stairs, and structural details to their foundations. Roof stairs now rise continuously toward the ridge, with outward-facing inverted eaves. Foliage no longer replaces roofs or walls; lake islets now have submerged bases.
- Repairs staircase approaches, direction, and support, and moves crystals away from the expedition gallery stairs.
- The five Overworld sites now share one placement set, selecting only one structure at each candidate and keeping neighboring candidates far enough apart. The Ancestral Chamber keeps its separate End placement.
- [Updated structure gallery](../guides/SPECIAL_STRUCTURES.md).

Update both client and server. These fixes appear in **new chunks**; structures already generated in your world are not rebuilt automatically. GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
