## A book you can hold

The Slimepedia becomes a dimensional book with raised cover, stitched spine, brass corners, pages, and bookmark. The player raises and opens it with both hands before reading, including offhand use, and other players see the pose. The item catalog receives dimensional SolarPunk models for tools, capsules, materials, rewards, and spawn eggs.
