## Droplet bodies and elemental effects

The 32 species, four bosses, and royal minion receive droplet-shaped bodies and distinct elemental effects. The visual direction includes head flames, liquid splashes, branching electricity, poison bubbles and vapor, stone over gel, and luminous liquid. Geometry and artwork are original.
