# Slime Evolution 0.7.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

## The catalog reaches 47 species

Fifteen acquired species expand the catalog from 32 to **47**, with **61 breeding pairs** and **14 metamorphoses**. Breeding adds Storm, Coral, Glass, Ferrofluid, Mycelium, and Caustic. Metamorphoses add Amber, Abyssal, Sculk, Solar, and Lunar. Advanced forms include Supercell, Hive, Aurora, and Eclipse. Each gains its own gel profile, accessories, atlas, and GeckoLib animations.
