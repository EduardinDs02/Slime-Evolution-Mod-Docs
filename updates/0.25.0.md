# 0.25.0 — Revised lineage concepts

Eleven original species anatomies now feature a geode cavity, full daisy, orbiting snowstorm, jellyfish bell and tentacles, dunes, cordyceps stalks, resonating forks, erupting volcano, green mineral crystals, phoenix wings and orbiting ash. Reef, Boreal and Abyssal Beacon were removed. Existing individuals migrate to Coral, Blizzard and Bioluminescent without losing identity or progression. The catalogue has 60 species, 79 breeding pairs and 21 metamorphoses.
