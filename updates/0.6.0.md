# Slime Evolution 0.6.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

## Refined elemental anatomy

Sixteen species receive visual refinements while preserving gel bodies, faces, and gameplay. Changes include additional crystals, broader Corrupted fissures, an ender-pearl-like End core, varied Floral flowers, icy Glacial Aquatic fins, a faceted Ice shell, and Magmatic volcanic crust. GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
