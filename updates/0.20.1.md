# Slime Evolution 0.20.1

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

Adds the **Creative Slime Wand**, usable only in Creative mode. Right-click a mod slime to grant 100 trust and maximum level (10). Find it in the mod's creative tab. It is reusable and has no recipe or loot source. Wild slimes bond to the player; existing caretakers and other individual data are preserved. [How to use](../guides/LEVELS.md#creative-slime-wand).

Update both client and server. GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
