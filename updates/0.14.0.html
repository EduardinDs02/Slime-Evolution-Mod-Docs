Eight renewed visual identities

Celestial gains internal constellations, orbital rings, and luminous wings. Energized Crystal gains varied crystals, pulsing veins, and electrical arcs. Soul gains small internal spirits and blue flames. Bog gains roots, cattails, vegetation, and bubbles. Mycelium gains branching filaments and mushrooms. Mud gains wet surfaces, drips, and landing splashes. Obsidian gains volcanic glass plates and fractures. Supercell gains layered clouds, lightning, and a focused tornado. These decorative changes preserve body shape, expressions, and gameplay and respect reduced motion.
