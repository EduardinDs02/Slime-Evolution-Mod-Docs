# Slime Evolution 0.19.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

## Food for your followers

The Royal Feed Bag stores five food stacks and uses the Gelatinous King's Royal Gel. Upgrade it with one Igneous Fragment from the Magmatic Heart for fifteen slots, preserving food and the bag's custom name. Carry it to feed only your own following slimes within sixteen blocks, respecting diets and normal meal cooldowns. [Recipes and behavior](../guides/FEED_BAGS.md).

## Companions keep responding at low energy

Low energy no longer blocks learned movement, autonomous Aggressive target selection, or basic attacks. Ranged companions without enough energy for their next special move into melee instead of waiting at firing distance. Special skills still cost their listed energy. A projectile already launched can land even when paying its cost left the caster below twenty energy.

Unfunded melee attacks now use the normal attack reach instead of requiring a physical collision after navigation stops. Winged followers can also take longer routes around nearby walls without expanding the checked loaded region. The changes were exercised with Ancestral, Celestial, and Eclipse against zombies and the Magmatic Heart, including transitions to Defensive and Passive.

Update the mod on client and server; GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
