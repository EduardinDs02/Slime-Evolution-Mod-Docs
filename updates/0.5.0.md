## Elemental details and boss encounters

Slimes receive expanded elemental accessories while retaining the gel shape and GeckoLib animation system. Bosses gain larger bodies, stronger phase-based attacks, expanded minions, and filled attack areas. Care, breeding, and rare metamorphoses continue to use persistent individual identities. Historical balance values belong to this release; consult the current guides before playing.
