# Slime Evolution 0.23.0

[Version history](README.md) · [Player guide](../README.md)

- Adds exterior detail to all six sites: recessed window frames, timbered gables, cornices, roof accents, paths, and planted borders.
- The Royal Court gains articulated side and rear elevations; the library gains timbered gables, exterior conservatory columns, and observatory cornices. The Ancestral Chamber gains upper gallery windows, a deeper portal, and spire bands.
- The Sanctuary gains roofed porticoes. The Lake cabin gains attic glazing and a lower porch roof, and compact trees replace crowns clipped at the template edges.
- Clears tower entrances blocked by window sills and removes an elevated shortcut crossing the Royal Court's upper room.
- The geode has a rounded shell within the template bounds. Placement preserves surrounding terrain while still excavating the occupied interior.
- Furnishes and organizes the interiors: Royal reading/dining rooms, library mezzanine and rear reading room, workstations, cabin and pavilion, and ancestral/expedition galleries. Adds guardrails while keeping circulation clear.
- [Updated gallery and comparison](../guides/SPECIAL_STRUCTURES.md).

Update both client and server. These changes appear in **new chunks**; existing structures are not rebuilt automatically. GeckoLib 4.9.3 remains required.
